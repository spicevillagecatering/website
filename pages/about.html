Our Story

A passion for authentic South Indian flavors

At Spice Village Catering, we bring the authentic taste of South India to your special events. As Ireland's premier South Indian catering service and Kerala catering specialists, our culinary journey is rooted in traditional recipes passed down through generations, combined with modern presentation and service excellence.

From the aromatic spices of Chettinad to the delicate flavors of Kerala traditional catering, from the hearty dishes of Andhra to the balanced tastes of Karnataka, we offer a complete South Indian culinary experience featuring authentic Kerala sadya catering, appam catering, dosa catering, and Indian biryani catering that will transport your guests to the vibrant streets of South India.

With over 15 years of experience serving the Irish community, we've perfected the art of bringing authentic South Indian cuisine to your doorstep. Our team of skilled chefs uses only the freshest ingredients and traditional cooking methods to ensure every dish is a masterpiece of flavor and authenticity.
